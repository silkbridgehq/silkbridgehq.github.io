Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

Short and plain: what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you request a quote or email us, we collect what you give us: your name, company, email, phone number (if you add it) and the details of what you want to buy.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your request, prepare your quote and talk to you about it. We may share product details (not your contact details) with factories to get prices.

Who else sees it

Quote requests from this website are delivered to our inbox by a form service. Our email is hosted by an Australian provider. We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists without asking.

Keeping it safe

We keep your details only as long as we need them for your enquiry or order, and take reasonable steps to protect them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email jacob@silkbridgehq.com.
