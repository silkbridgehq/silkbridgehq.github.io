FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a minimum order?

Each factory sets its own minimum order quantity (MOQ). We'll tell you the MOQ with every quote, and look for factories that suit your volumes.

How long does it take?

It depends on the product. As a rough guide, allow a few weeks for samples, then production, then sea freight to Australia. We give you a timeline with every quote.

Who do I pay?

You pay the factory directly for the goods, using a secure method. You pay our commission separately, only if you order. We'll never ask you to pay a factory into a new or changed bank account without checking it first.

What about duty, GST and customs?

Many goods made in China can enter Australia duty-free under the China–Australia Free Trade Agreement with the right proof of origin, and GST applies to imports. Every product is different, so we'll flag what applies and you should confirm the details with your customs broker or accountant.

Can you put our logo or brand on products?

Yes. Many factories offer custom printing, labels and packaging. It usually means a higher minimum order, and we'll include that in the quote.

What if the goods aren't right?

That's why we use samples and inspect before shipping. Problems are much easier to fix at the factory than after goods arrive in Australia.
