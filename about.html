About

A bridge between Australia and the factory floor.

Silk Bridge HQ (丝桥, "silk bridge") is a Melbourne sourcing business founded by Jacob Abbas.

Buying direct from China can save Australian businesses a lot of money. But finding a factory you can trust, checking what you're getting and getting it here safely takes time most businesses don't have.

That's the gap Silk Bridge HQ fills. We do the factory side: find the right manufacturer, check they're real, get samples, inspect the goods and organise delivery. You make the decisions and stay in control of every payment.

We're a new business, and we're upfront about it. We focus on eight product categories, check every supplier properly, and only get paid when you order. If we're not the right fit for what you need, we'll tell you.
